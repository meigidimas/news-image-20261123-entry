## The day in the news

- **COP31 collapses** — the longest climate summit on record ends in Antalya with no pact, after running more than 50 hours over time, split over fossil fuels.
- **Kyiv says it sank a warship off Libya** — Zelensky says seven sea drones sank the Russian corvette *Merkury* at Benghazi.
- **Huang is back at Nvidia** — shares jump 31% as the co-founder returns as chief executive; the Nasdaq climbs 6.5%.
- **Russian strikes kill 15 near Kyiv** — missiles and drones hit homes around the capital.
- **24 dead in Indonesia factory fire** — the toll is confirmed as the search goes on for about 30 missing after the candy-factory blaze in Banten.
- **U.S. hits Iran's shadow banks** — two dozen exchange houses targeted; $15bn in flows flagged.
- **BepiColombo safe in Mercury orbit** — ESA confirms the probe is stable after a glitch cut its arrival burn short.
- **India gives Nepal $1.62bn to rebuild** — aid to recover from August's floods.

Every story was verified against reporting from Reuters, AP, CNN, ESA and the U.S. State Department dated 23 November 2026.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered front page `entry.png` (1024 x 1024, under 1 MiB) into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. The run is deterministic: the same bytes every time.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
